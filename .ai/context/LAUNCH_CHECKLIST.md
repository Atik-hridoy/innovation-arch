# Launch Checklist

Final checklist before deploying to production.

## 🚀 Pre-Launch Validation

- **[ ] Meta tags**: Check title tags, meta descriptions, and Open Graph graphics.
- **[ ] Analytics**: Verify tracking setups and event conversion indicators.
- **[ ] Contact Forms**: Test lead generation inputs with real data.
- **[ ] Sitemap & Robots**: Verify sitemap mapping and crawler rules.
- **[ ] Favicons**: Check standard branding favicons on all devices.
- **[ ] Performance Audit**: Ensure production Lighthouse metrics score > 95.
- **[ ] Visual Quality check**: Execute final visual regression audit.
