# SEO Strategy Guide

Maintain high visibility search ranking with proper SEO configurations.

## 🏷️ Metadata & Semantic Headers
- Title tag: "Innovative Arc | Premium Digital Product Studio" (Maximum 60 characters).
- Description tag: "Innovative Arc is an elite digital product design and engineering studio. We build immersive, high-performance web products." (Maximum 160 characters).
- Semantic headers: Maintain exactly one structural `<h1>` element per page.

## 🖼️ Open Graph & Rich Previews
- `og:title`: "Innovative Arc | Premium Digital Product Studio"
- `og:description`: "Elite digital product engineering and design."
- `og:image`: Point to `/assets/og-image.png` (Dimensions: `1200x630px`).
- Include `twitter:card` (large image layout) tags.

## ⚙️ Configuration Files
- **Sitemap**: `/sitemap.xml` mapping active routes (`/`, `/services`, `/about`).
- **Robots.txt**: `/robots.txt` specifying allowed crawling agents.
