# Content Strategy Guide

This document maintains standard brand messaging across all sections.

## ✍️ Key Headlines & Copy

### Hero Section
- **Headline**: "We engineer digital products of outstanding craftsmanship."
- **Sub-headline**: "A premium product engineering studio designing next-generation digital identities."

### CTA Lead Section
- **Headline**: "Let's build something future-focused."
- **Sub-headline**: "Partner with an elite engineering studio to craft your digital reality."

## 📣 Tone Guidelines
- Keep headings bold and action-oriented.
- Avoid passive voice. Prefer active, high-impact phrasing (e.g. "We build" instead of "We are building").
- Use descriptive tooltips, friendly input error microcopy, and clear loading messages.
