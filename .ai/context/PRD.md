# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Innovative Arc

## 🎯 Objectives
Build an immersive, Awwwards-nomination worthy website that showcases Innovative Arc as an elite digital product engineering company. The primary goal is lead generation.

## 📄 Site Structure & Pages
- **Hero**: Atmospheric, cinematic entrance, showing craftsmanship.
- **Services**: Detail engineering, UI/UX design, and product strategy capabilities.
- **Process**: Chronological interactive visual timeline mapping the studio's workflow.
- **Featured Work**: Immersive project grid/slider with custom magnetic reveals.
- **FAQ**: Clean interactive accordion answering client onboarding questions.
- **CTA / Lead Gen**: Futuristic premium contact form to convert visitors.

## 🛠️ Functional Requirements
- **Fluid Layout**: Standard 1440px desktop container with fluid mobile responsiveness down to 320px.
- **Transitions**: Smooth page routing and layout animations linked to scrolling.
- **Validation**: High-performance contact form with input validation and feedback micro-animations.

## 📊 KPIs & Success Criteria
- **Performance**: Lighthouse score of >95 on desktop and mobile.
- **User Engagement**: High conversion rate on CTA contact form, optimized scroll depth, and smooth animations with zero frame drops.
